# TCK-105 · Pods OOMKilled every ~6 hours
**Customer:** Aldermoor Capital · **Reporter:** Luis Ortega, Platform Engineer · **Version:** 2.4.0

> Since 2.4.0 our RiskCube pods restart every 5-7 hours. Memory grows in a straight line during the day,
> **even at lunchtime when almost nobody is connected**. Our dashboards auto-refresh every 30 seconds.
> We had no issue with 2.3.2 on the same pods (4 GiB limit).
>
> ```
> $ kubectl describe pod riskcube-7c4b8-m9tz
>     Last State:  Terminated
>       Reason:    OOMKilled
>       Exit Code: 137
> ```
> Grafana: `jvm_memory_used_bytes{area="heap"}` goes from 1.1 GB at 08:00 to 3.9 GB at 14:00.
>
> Sample of our access logs:
> ```
> GET /api/v1/aggregates?groupBy=DESK&_=1790668830417 status=200 took=41ms
> GET /api/v1/aggregates?groupBy=DESK&_=1790668860388 status=200 took=38ms
> GET /api/v1/aggregates?groupBy=CURRENCY&_=1790668860395 status=200 took=44ms
> ```
