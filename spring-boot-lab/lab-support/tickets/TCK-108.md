**Customer:** Northbridge Bank · **Reporter:** Tom Becker, Integration Team · **Version:** 2.4.0

> Our nightly job loads the day's trades with `POST /api/v1/trades` using the `admin` technical account.

> can still call `GET` endpoints and `/actuator`. Yesterday's trades are missing from today's risk report.
